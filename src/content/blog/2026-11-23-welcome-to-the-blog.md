Hi, and welcome to the WE AutoPilot blog.

Instagram is great for photos and Discord is great for "meeting moved to 6". Neither is a good place to explain how a planner picks a path or why a sensor mount cracked. That's what this page is for.

## What goes here

Anything a team wants to share about building a car that drives itself:

- what you worked on this week, and what's next
- a problem you got stuck on and how you got unstuck
- something you learned that the next person shouldn't have to learn the hard way
- recaps of workshops, build nights and competitions

Posts can be short. A few paragraphs and one good photo is plenty.

> note: short and specific beats long and polished

## Who can post

Any team. Every post is tagged with the team that wrote it (or "Club" for posts about all of us), so you can filter the blog to just Perception, just Build, and so on.

If you're a lead and you want to write something, the how-to lives next to the posts in our GitHub repo, in `src/content/blog/README.md`. You write one Markdown file and open a pull request. No web development needed.

See you in the next post.
